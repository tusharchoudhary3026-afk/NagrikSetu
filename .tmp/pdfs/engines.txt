ENGINE 11 — Voice Complaint Intake & Speech-to-Text
Status: TRY
Purpose: Allow citizens to file complaints by speaking instead of typing.
Workflow
 Citizen taps the microphone button.
 Speech is converted into editable text.
 Citizen reviews the transcript before submitting.
 Existing NyayaFlow NLP pipeline processes the confirmed text.
